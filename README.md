# Awesome MagicGrid MBSE [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

A curated list of resources for the MagicGrid MBSE methodology: official material, the Book of Knowledge, papers and case studies, talks, training, example models, and tool support. For SysML v2 language and tooling, see the sister list under Related Methodologies.

> This list is maintained independently. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Dassault Systemes or No Magic. MagicGrid is a methodology brand of No Magic, now part of Dassault Systemes. Every entry links to a legitimate public source; this list does not host copies of the MagicGrid Book of Knowledge or other copyrighted material.

## Papers and Case Studies

- [Architecture meta-model for MagicGrid (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1002/iis2.13284) - INCOSE 2024 paper proposing a systems architecture meta-model for the MagicGrid framework by No Magic methodology staff (Aleksandraviciene, Strolia, Morkevicius).
- [Extending MagicGrid for virtual prototyping (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1145/3652620.3686249) - ACM 2024 paper on extending MagicGrid to support virtual prototyping for early system performance V&V.
- [Flight-control requirements case study (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-97-0550-4_3) - Springer 2024 case study on a MagicGrid-based requirements development process for a flight control system.
- [Mainstream MBSE methodologies survey (2022)](https://doi.org/10.3233/faia220529) - Survey from 2022 comparing mainstream MBSE methodologies from a modeling-practice view, including MagicGrid.
- [MBSE Grid hydroelectric health-monitoring case (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-20490-6_32) - Springer 2022 case applying MBSE Grid operational analysis to a hydroelectric group health monitoring unit.
- [MBSE Grid origin paper (2017)](https://doi.org/10.1002/j.2334-5837.2017.00350.x) - INCOSE 2017 paper introducing MBSE Grid, the precursor to MagicGrid, by No Magic methodology staff.
- [Space remote-sensing case study (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-33-4102-9_35) - Springer 2020 case study applying MagicGrid methodology to a space-borne remote sensing imaging system.
- [SysML v1 vs v2 in MagicGrid scope (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1002/iis2.13257) - INCOSE 2024 paper comparing SysML v1 and SysML v2 application within the MagicGrid framework by No Magic methodology staff.
- [SysML v2 solution architecture with MagicGrid (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1002/iis2.70000) - INCOSE 2025 paper exploring SysML v2 for solution architecture development with the MagicGrid framework.
- [System V&V using MagicGrid (INSIGHT 2023)](https://doi.org/10.1002/inst.12429) - INSIGHT 2023 article on a system verification and validation approach using the MagicGrid framework by No Magic methodology staff (Morkevicius et al.).
- [System V&V using MagicGrid (IS 2022)](https://doi.org/10.1002/iis2.12962) - INCOSE IS 2022 paper on a system verification and validation approach using the MagicGrid framework by No Magic methodology staff (Morkevicius et al.).
- [Towards a Common SE Methodology (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1002/j.2334-5837.2020.00713.x) - INCOSE 2020 paper toward a common systems engineering methodology covering a complete development process grounded in MBSE Grid.
